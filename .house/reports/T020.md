## Goal
Implement D-026: account numbers keep a printed `PL` on OCR pages, digits only on text-layer pages (tasks/T020-pl-prefix-scans.md).

- `odw2json/engine/engine.py`: `_normalise` takes the candidate page source (`text.sources[c.page]`) and passes it to page-aware normalisers only. No class or field names in the engine.
- `recipes/przelew.yaml`: both account fields use `iban_pl_as_printed`; IBAN checksum validation unchanged.
- `docs/ADDING_A_DOCUMENT_CLASS.md`: new normaliser row.
- `tests/test_normaliser_page_source.py`: text page, OCR with PL, OCR without PL, wrong length, validator (written first).
